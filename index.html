Common questions

How does Cuvori make money if there's no commission?

Cuvori is free for clients and editors while the marketplace grows. Optional paid features for professionals may come later; the 0% commission on project prices stays.

How do I pay the professional?

You agree payment terms in the contract and pay them directly — bank transfer, PayPal or whatever you both prefer. Integrated payments are planned.

Why do editors need an invite?

Invites keep the quality of professional profiles high while Cuvori is small. Any account can browse and hire; only creating a professional profile needs an invite.

What if something goes wrong with a project?

Timestamped review comments and the written contract make it clear what was agreed. If you still can't resolve it, contact support from the Settings page.

  • Project scope and deliverables
  • Price and whether it's per project, hourly or daily
  • Timeline and milestones
  • Number of revision rounds
